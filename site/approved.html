    What an approval means

    We check a company’s paperwork, visit one of its job sites or its shop, and score what we see against a written standard. A record shows only what we checked or saw ourselves. Nothing on it is the company’s own claim.

    If a company shows you an AT Legacy Services approval, look up its number here. This page on atlegacyservices.com is the proof. Some approved companies aren’t named here, so if you can’t find one, ask us and we’ll tell you where it stands.
