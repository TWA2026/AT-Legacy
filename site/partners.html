How we place you

  1. You pick your tier

    On the application, tell us where your pricing usually lands.

  2. We check the fit

    We look at your years in business, the warranty you give, and how soon you can schedule. If they point to a different tier, we talk before your first request.

  3. We verify your company

    State registration, your license if your trade needs one, liability insurance, and references from local customers. The checks are the same for every tier.

  4. You hold one tier

    Requests for that tier come to you. If your pricing, warranty, or experience changes, ask us to move you.
