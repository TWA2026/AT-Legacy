How you get approved

We send requests only to companies on our approved vendor list. Getting on it takes four steps.

  1. Apply

    Fill in the form at the bottom of this page.

  2. We check your paperwork

    State registration, your license if your trade needs one, liability insurance, and references from local customers.

  3. We visit a job

    We come to one of your job sites, or your shop, and score what we see against our written standard. Every company has to meet the basics, such as a safe, clean site and work that matches the quote.

  4. You go on the list

    Meet the basics and you’re an approved vendor, with a certificate you can show your customers. Customers can read the standard too. If you want, we name you on our approved companies page. We come back for another visit to keep it current. This is our own standard, not a state license.

How we set your tier

Every approved vendor meets the same basics. Past those, the visit gives points for what customers pay more for: years in business, your warranty, and how fast you start and answer. Your points suggest your tier. We also look at where you price, and the final call is ours.
