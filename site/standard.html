Our standard

What a company has to show us before it can quote your job. This is our own standard, not a state license.

Paperwork we check first

  • Registration. An active business registration with the State of Florida.
  • License. A state license for trades that need one, such as pools, electrical, plumbing, pest control, and fertilizing.
  • Insurance. Proof of liability insurance.
  • References. Local customers who recommend them.

What we look for at the visit

We go to one of the company’s job sites, or its shop, and score what we see.

      It doesn’t last forever

      An approval runs out. We visit each company again to keep it current. A company that stops meeting the standard comes off the list.

      What it doesn’t mean

      Our checks say what we found on the days we looked. They aren’t a guarantee of a company’s work. Each quote we send you shows that company’s record, so you can check it yourself before you hire.
