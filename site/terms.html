The plain rules for using this service. Last updated November 21, 2026.

What this service is

AT Legacy Services is a quote service run by AT Legacy Group LLC. You describe a job, and we collect quotes for it from independent local companies. It is free for homeowners. By sending a request you agree to these terms.

Quotes and hiring

  • Each quote comes from the company named on it. That company sets the price, the timeline, and the warranty.
  • You don’t have to accept any quote.
  • If you hire a company, your agreement is with that company. You pay it directly, and it is responsible for the work.
  • AT Legacy Services isn’t the contractor on a partner company’s job and isn’t a party to your agreement with it.

What our checks mean

Before a company can quote, we check its registration, its license where its trade needs one, its proof of insurance, and its references, and we visit one of its job sites or its shop. Our standard lists what we look for.

These checks say what we found on the days we looked. They aren’t a guarantee of a company’s work, and a license or an insurance policy can lapse after we check it. Before you hire, confirm the company’s license and insurance yourself and get your agreement in writing.

Our fee

You pay us nothing. The company you hire pays us a finder’s fee.

Repairs and upkeep by our own crew

Small repairs and upkeep are quoted and done by AT Legacy Services itself. Our written quote for that work sets its price and terms.

Your part

  • Give accurate information, and ask for quotes only on a property you own or have permission to arrange work for.
  • Add only pictures and files you have the right to share. You keep them, and you let us show them to the companies quoting your job.
  • Don’t use the service to send anything unlawful or abusive.

For partner companies

  • There is no charge to apply, to receive a request, or to quote. You owe a finder’s fee only on a job a customer chooses you for through us.
  • The fee is . The terms shown on your quote page when you send a price are the ones that apply to that job.
  • We bill the fee after the customer chooses you. It is due days after that day.
  • If the job falls through before any work starts, tell us and no fee is owed.
  • No fee is owed on work that is paid for by a property insurance claim.
  • What you put in a quote is your own offer to the customer, and you stand behind it.
  • We can pause or end an approval when a company stops meeting our standard.

We work to pass along accurate information, but the quotes, the work, and the conduct of a partner company are that company’s own.
