What Florida weather does to a house

Heat, humidity, and rain wear on the same few spots every year. These are the ones we fix most.

  • Summer humidity Doors swell, stick, and stop latching. Door adjustment
  • Heat and sun Caulk dries out and pulls away from tubs, counters, and trim. Fresh caulk
  • Rainy season Gutters fill with leaves and overflow at the roof edge. Gutter cleaning
  • Shade and damp Algae and mildew darken driveways, walkways, and patios. Pressure washing
  • Daily wear Paint scuffs and fades on walls, doors, and trim. Paint touch-ups

Questions

How do I get a price?

Fill out the quote form and attach a few photos to the email it creates. We reply with a written price, and nothing starts until you approve it.

Can you do several small jobs in one visit?

Yes. List everything in the quote form and we’ll price it as one visit.

What jobs don’t you take?

Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, and structural work, and any job that needs a permit. If your job needs a licensed trade, we’ll tell you up front.

Are you a licensed contractor?

No. Florida doesn’t require a state contractor license for the jobs we take, such as painting, caulking, mounting, assembly, and pressure washing. We don’t take work that does.

Do you work on rental properties?

Yes. We handle move-out touch-ups and punch lists for landlords and property managers.
