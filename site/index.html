What verified means

A company can’t quote through us until it passes every check that applies to its trade, and a site visit.

  • Registration Active business registration with the State of Florida.
  • License State license confirmed for trades that need one, such as pools, electrical, plumbing, pest control, and fertilizing.
  • Insurance Proof of liability insurance on file.
  • References Recommended by local customers before we add them.
  • Site visit We visit one of their job sites, or their shop, and score it against our own written standard.

Questions

Does it cost me anything?

No. Requests and quotes are free for homeowners. The company you hire pays us a finder’s fee.

Who does the work?

The company you choose. Each quote comes from that company, and you hire and pay them directly. AT Legacy Services doesn’t set the price and isn’t the contractor.

Do I have to pick one of the quotes?

No. Compare them and hire any of the three, or none.

How long do quotes take?

It depends on the job. Regular services can often be priced from your description and photos. Bigger projects like pools and fences usually need a site visit first.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re starting in Land O’ Lakes and nearby Pasco County communities.

What about repairs and upkeep?

Small repairs, mounting, and pressure washing are done by our own crew, so you get one quote from us instead of three.
