Privacy

What we collect, who sees it, and how to have it removed. Last updated November 22, 2026.

The short version

  • Companies quoting your job see the job, not you. Your name, phone number, and email go only to the company you choose.
  • We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Ask us to delete your request and we will.

What we collect

  • Your request. The service you need, the ZIP code and city where the work is, your timing, and how you describe the job.
  • Your contact details. Your name, your phone number, your email if you give it, and how you want to be reached.
  • Pictures and files you add. We shrink each picture and remove what the camera wrote into it, including where it was taken.
  • Messages. What you send us from the contact page.
  • Company applications. A company that applies gives us its name, a contact person, a phone number and email, its license number, and the areas it serves.
  • Link activity. We note the time a private quote link is opened, so we know it arrived. We don’t record where you were.

The companies that host this site and keep it safe handle your connection details, such as your internet address, to deliver pages and stop abuse.

How we use it

To write your job brief, collect quotes for it, show them to you, and put you in touch with the company you choose. We also use it to answer your questions and to keep records of the jobs we arrange.

Who sees it

  • Companies quoting. They see your job brief and the pictures you added, under plain names such as Picture 2. They don’t see your name or contact details.
  • The company you choose. When you choose a company, it gets your name and contact details so it can schedule the work.
  • Documents. A survey or a plan often shows a name or an address, so we look at each document before any company sees it.
  • An AI writing service. We may use one to turn your job description into the brief. Your name, phone number, and email are never sent to it.
  • Our service providers. The companies that host the site and store its data for us.

We don’t sell your information or share it for advertising. We may share it when the law requires us to.

Cookies and tracking

The public pages use no advertising or analytics trackers. The service that protects the site from abuse may set a short-lived cookie to tell people from automated traffic.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep a request and its quotes as a record of the job. You can remove a picture or file yourself on your request page until the request is closed. To see, correct, or delete what we hold about you, call or text {{phone}}, or use the contact page. We delete it unless the law or our accounts require us to keep a record.

Children

This service is for adults arranging work on a property. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes and contact
